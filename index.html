Download for Windows ↓

This is a testing preview. The Windows installer is unsigned. The Android app is release-signed. Use these files only if they meet your organisation’s installation policy.

Your download is starting. Windows may check with you twice.
  1. Keep the file if your browser asks. In Edge, choose … → Keep → Keep anyway. In Chrome, choose Keep.
  2. Open it from your downloads. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info → Run anyway, then Yes.
  3. Accept the licence and choose Install. PartTrace opens in your browser when it is ready.
  1. 1
    Run the installer

    Open the downloaded file, accept the licence and choose Install. It takes about a minute.

  2. 2
    Make it your workshop

    PartTrace opens in your browser. Create your account and save your recovery code.

  3. 3
    Bring your team in

    Set up backups, then scan a setup code to connect your first phone.
